Who I Am

Profile

“A strategy and enablement professional building stronger cultures through stakeholder alignment, capability development, and meaningful communication.”

I’m Shubhada — a Product Strategist, Internal Trainer, and Customer Excellence professional with experience across India, Germany, and Slovenia. My journey started in Mechanical Engineering, expanded through analytics and strategy, and evolved into work focused on transformation, learning, and human growth.

Today, I work at the intersection of business transformation, customer excellence, training design, and stakeholder engagement. I enjoy building scalable initiatives, facilitating workshops, and creating structured programs that help teams perform better together.

Alongside my corporate work, I actively coach and mentor students and early professionals on communication, confidence, leadership mindset, and career readiness. Outside work, I’m a dancer, traveller, reader, swimmer, and abstract photographer who is endlessly curious about people and cultures.
